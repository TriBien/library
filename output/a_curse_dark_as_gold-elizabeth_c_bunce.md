### My New Goal: Dare to Fail, Dare to Grow

Hello, everyone.

Today, I'd like to talk about my day.

I just got back home from a vacation in France, and today, I made an important decision. I've decided to look for a job at a product company where I can apply my technical expertise and contribute to building great products.

I know this journey won't be easy. I'll need to update my CV, prepare for interviews, attend multiple interview rounds, and deal with many challenges along the way.

But I think the biggest challenge isn't the technical preparation itself. It's overcoming my own psychological barriers, especially the fear of failure.

I realize that I need to practice becoming comfortable with failure. Instead of seeing failure as something negative, I want to treat it as a feedback loop that helps me learn, improve, and become more resilient over time.

This is similar to how we build reliable software systems. We observe what goes wrong, analyze the feedback, identify the root causes, and make improvements. Over time, the system becomes stronger and more robust.

I want to apply the same principle to my own personal development.

My new goal is simple: dare to fail, learn from my mistakes, and keep moving forward. I want to develop the courage to face difficulties, overcome challenges, and become mentally stronger through experience.

I know there will be setbacks, but I don't want the fear of failure to stop me from taking action.

This is the strategy I want to follow in this new chapter of my life: embrace challenges, learn continuously, and become a better version of myself every day.

That's all for today. Thank you for listening.
