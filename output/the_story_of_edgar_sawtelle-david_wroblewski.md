# My Future Career Direction: From Software Engineering to AI Engineering

Hello, everyone.

Today, I'd like to share a little bit about my plans for the future.

I've already started shifting my focus from traditional software engineering toward AI engineering.

However, I don't see this as a complete replacement of traditional software engineering. Instead, I believe the best approach is to start with a hybrid model.

On one side, I'll continue building on my traditional software engineering foundation, including backend development with Java, frontend development with React.js, and other established programming technologies.

On the other side, I'll invest more time and effort in developing my AI engineering skills. My goal is to learn how to integrate AI capabilities into traditional software systems and gradually move toward AI-native application development.

I believe this will become an important direction for the software industry.

In the future, software engineers may use AI-native frameworks, platforms, and development methodologies to build applications, just as we adopted cloud-native technologies in the past.

I also expect AI to become increasingly integrated across different layers of the technology stack, from infrastructure and platforms to applications and end-user experiences.

However, I don't think AI should be applied everywhere blindly.

The right approach should depend on the specific context, business requirements, and use cases.

Sometimes, traditional software engineering is still the best solution. In other situations, we can leverage AI, large language models, or even simple manual processes.

The key is to understand the problem first and then choose the most appropriate technology to solve it.

Ultimately, my goal is to use my software engineering experience and AI capabilities to contribute to the technology industry, build useful solutions, and create meaningful positive impacts on society.

That's my plan for the future.

Thank you for listening.
